01 — Validation

A real, large problem that just got more serious.

Since July 1, 2026, Brazil's DeCripto rule (IN RFB 2,291/2025) replaced the old IN 1,888. Anyone trading outside Brazilian exchanges, on DEXs, self-custody wallets or foreign platforms, must report every month once they pass R$35,000. International reporting is expanding through the OECD's CARF standard, with participating jurisdictions committed to begin exchanges in 2027, 2028 or 2029.

02 — Impact

Why this matters beyond Brazil.

CARF creates a shared international reporting framework. We start with Brazil; reusable ingestion and transaction records could support expansion, while each country's tax rules would require separate implementation and validation.

Traders

Less time assembling transaction records, with calculations and unresolved items available for review.

Accountants

Reports designed to connect calculations to source events, prices and applied rules for review.

Ecosystem

Clear compliance lowers the fear of using Solana and Hyperliquid and brings more people on-chain.

Society

Help users organize their own records and understand their reporting obligations.

03 — Architecture

How the system works.

From wallet to report in five stages. Every integration is read-only: users share public addresses and we never touch private keys or funds.

01 · Ingestion

Solana history through Helius; Hyperliquid fills, funding, deposits, transfers and airdrops through its public Info API.

02 · Pricing

Historical USD prices, taken from the transaction itself when a stablecoin is involved and from CoinGecko otherwise, converted to BRL with the Central Bank's PTAX rate.

03 · Tax engine

Average cost basis per token, the R$35K monthly exemption and an estimated tax at a flat 15%. Every number shows the events, price and rule behind it. The official DeCripto layout, GCAP and DARF outputs are next.

04 + 05 · Agent and proof

The tax engine calculates; the AI agent explains its results and cites the transactions. Each final report's SHA-256 hash is recorded in a Solana memo transaction (devnet today), and anyone can check the file on a public page. It verifies file integrity, not tax correctness.

04 — Progress

Where we are today.

We started Orbix Declare in late September 2026, during the hackathon. Everything below was built within the judging window and is running in production.

Done

  • Sign-in by signing a message with Phantom or Solflare, plus e-mail code, Google and GitHub. No passwords.
  • Read-only ingestion from a public address: Solana through Helius; Hyperliquid fills, funding, deposits, transfers and airdrops.
  • Pricing in reais at the Central Bank's PTAX rate of the trade day.
  • Tax engine: average cost per token, monthly exemption, perps and funding, with the origin of every number on screen.
  • Monthly report: CSV, review package for the accountant, and new versions when data changes.
  • SHA-256 hash of each final report recorded on Solana (devnet) and a public verification page.
  • AI agent that explains the calculation and cites transactions, with a rules-based fallback.
  • Postgres with Row Level Security, private file storage, 204 automated tests.

Not done yet

  • DeCripto file in the tax authority's official layout. Today we prepare the data for it.
  • GCAP and DARF outputs.
  • Report hash on Solana mainnet. It runs on devnet today.
  • Progressive tax brackets. The estimate uses a flat 15%.
  • Validation sessions with traders and an accountant.

06 — Proposal

Tax compliance for people who live on-chain.

We build for Brazilian users of Solana and Hyperliquid who need to turn complex activity into reviewable tax records. Our focus is reducing recurring manual work and explaining how each calculation was produced.

PRODUCT SCREENS · DEMO MODE WITH SAMPLE DATA

Step 01

Sign in with your wallet

Sign a message with Phantom or Solflare and add your Hyperliquid addresses. Read-only, no private keys.

Step 02

The engine calculates

Every event in reais at the official daily rate, with average cost basis and exemptions applied.

Step 03

Review and download

The month's report and a review package for your accountant, with the agent explaining every line and a hash recorded on Solana.

ProblemBrazilian DeFi users must assemble records across wallets and trading platforms. Existing services cover this market, but documented workflows still involve recurring file uploads and manual clarification of some operations.
SolutionA working MVP combining on-chain ingestion, a tax engine in reais and an AI agent that explains results for review, with each report's hash recorded on Solana for integrity.
DifferentiatorOur differentiation hypothesis: less recurring manual work for Solana and Hyperliquid users, plus explanations tied to source events and calculation rules. Validate through real user workflows.
Business modelFreemium: free with up to 3 wallets and 20 agent questions per month. Pro and accountant plans are planned; pricing will be set after validation with users. Accountants are our main growth channel.
First customersThe Superteam Brasil community and Solana and Hyperliquid traders; then accounting firms.
Why nowThe transition to DeCripto increases the need for organized records. We see an opportunity to improve the recurring workflow for Brazilian Solana and Hyperliquid users within an established market.
Post-accelerator roadmapPro plan paid in USDC via Solana Pay, on-chain pricing with Jupiter or Pyth, a composable on-chain attestation of each report that other apps can verify, and a tax API for wallets, exchanges and fintechs.
VisionStart with Brazilian DeFi users, then expand reusable data infrastructure with locally validated tax rules.
Our askA spot in the Colosseum accelerator to validate our focused MVP with users and accountants, measure the reduction in manual work and build toward launch.
